Welcome to **404 Network — Club Not Found**, a student organization at the **University of Wisconsin–Green Bay** for students interested in computer science, software development, technology, and building cool things together.

We provide a place for students to **collaborate on projects, connect with other developers, share technical knowledge, and gain experience outside of the classroom**.

---

## `404.about`

### Who We Are

404 Network is a community for students interested in **computer science and technology**.

Our goal is to create an environment where members can learn from one another, experiment with new technologies, and turn ideas into real projects.

Whether you're writing your first program or trying to figure out why something only breaks in production, you're welcome here.

```text
Experience Level: Any
Curiosity Required: Yes
Perfect Code Required: Absolutely Not
Semicolons: Situational
```

## `404.mission`

### What We Do

Our club focuses on hands-on learning and collaboration through:

- 💻 **Collaborative programming projects**
- 🧠 **Peer-led technical sessions**
- 🌐 **Web and software development**
- 🤝 **Networking with fellow CS students**
- 🔧 **Git and GitHub collaboration**
- 🎨 **UI/UX design**
- 📚 **Learning new languages, frameworks, and tools**
- 📈 **Portfolio and résumé development**
- 🧪 **Experimenting with new technology**
- 🐛 **Turning bugs into undocumented features**

The goal isn't just to write code.

It's to learn how to **plan, design, build, test, document, and maintain projects as a team**.
